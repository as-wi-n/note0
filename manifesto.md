# note0 Manifesto

Knowledge should not disappear when students graduate.

Knowledge should not be locked inside private groups, temporary cloud links, or abandoned drives.

note0 exists to build a community-maintained archive of educational knowledge.

Anyone can contribute. Anyone can improve. Anyone can learn.

We believe educational materials should be organized, discoverable, preserved, and shared for future learners.

Communities, not corporations, should decide what knowledge is valuable.

Our goal is not merely to host files, but to create a living knowledge commons that survives institutions, platforms, and generations of students.

Knowledge belongs to everyone.
